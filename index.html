NC Physics Organization

NCPhO is run by physics researchers and USA Physics Olympiad medalists at the North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics who are passionate about increasing engagement in physics.

Our online olympiad will run two times a year and anyone can enter. First place wins $100, and three more competitors win $20 each in a random draw.

NC Physics Olympiad

The North Carolina Physics Olympiad is a free online contest we run a few times a year, and anyone can sign up. All of physics is fair game, but the problems favor critical thinking, so you can win even if you haven't taken every course yet.
